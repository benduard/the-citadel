                                  What this tile is honest about

                                  • The ladder is a scale
                                    Nine tiers of ratios somebody picked. Printed in full on the Ranks page. It is not a measurement of you.
                                  • Recovery is an estimate from volume
                                    It reads the sets you logged and nothing else. No heart rate, no sleep, no wearable. It cannot see how you actually feel, which is what Check in is for.
                                  • One rep max is a formula
                                    Epley, off sets of 1 to 12. Always labelled as an estimate, never shown as a lift you did.
                                  • Haptics do nothing on iPhone
                                    iOS Safari has no vibration API. The call is wired for Android and quietly does nothing here.
                                  • Nothing leaves this frame
                                    The tile is sealed. It cannot fetch, it holds no key, and it can only read and write its own slot in your vault.
