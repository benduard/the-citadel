How XP works

Every tick is 10 XP. Finishing a quest pays 25 XP plus 5 XP for each tick it asked for, so a bigger target is worth more.

Level 1 starts at 0 XP. Each level costs 100 XP more than the one before: 100 to reach level 2, another 200 to reach level 3, and on from there.

A streak is a day you ticked at least once. Miss a whole day and it starts again from your next tick. Your XP and your best streak stay exactly where they were.

Ticks do not undo. Removing a quest keeps the XP it earned, and a quest you already finished still counts in your finished total.

XP comes only from quests you tick here. This tile is sealed, it cannot read your other tiles or your ledger, so nothing is ever awarded automatically.
